  extended_description: |
    ## The Stats Duck

    A statistical computing toolkit that brings common statistical work into
    DuckDB SQL. Every function is a streaming aggregate or a scalar function,
    so it runs inside `GROUP BY` and window frames over large tables, and the
    same build runs in DuckDB-WASM in the browser.

    **What is included**

    - **Descriptive statistics**: `summary_stats`, `table_one` (the clinical
      "Table 1"), `corr_matrix`, `meta`.
    - **Hypothesis tests**: one-sample, two-sample and paired t-tests, one-way
      ANOVA, chi-square, Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon signed-rank,
      Kolmogorov-Smirnov, Shapiro-Wilk, Anderson-Darling, Jarque-Bera, and
      correlation tests (Pearson, Spearman, Kendall).
    - **Distribution functions**: R-style `d`/`p`/`q`/`r` quartets for 15+
      families (normal, t, chi-square, F, gamma, beta, exponential, Weibull,
      log-normal, Poisson, negative binomial, hypergeometric, and more), plus
      `ptukey` and `qtukey` for the studentized range.
    - **Regression**: the `lm` and `lm_summary` table functions and the
      `lm_fit` aggregate (one model per `GROUP BY`) with classical, HC0 to HC3
      heteroskedasticity-consistent, and CR0/CR1 cluster-robust standard
      errors. A rank-deficient design is fitted with the aliased columns
      reported NULL, as R does. `glm_fit` fits generalized linear models by
      iteratively reweighted least squares for the gaussian, binomial, poisson
      and gamma families and their links, with the same covariance options.
    - **Inference after a fit**: `lin_hyp` tests a general linear hypothesis
      L·β = 0 on any fit's coefficients and covariance (contrasts, joint
      tests, Type III sums of squares). `emmeans` computes estimated marginal
      means and their pairwise differences, with Tukey, Bonferroni, Holm,
      Hochberg, BH or BY adjustment.
    - **Grammar of graphics**: `VISUALIZE … DRAW <mark>` returns a Vega-Lite v5
      spec plus the SQL needed to feed it.
    - **Statistical file formats**: readers and writers for SAS
      (`.sas7bdat`, `.xpt`), SPSS (`.sav`, `.por`) and Stata files, via
      ReadStat.
    - **Resampling and correction**: `bootstrap`, and `adjust_p` with the
      Bonferroni, Holm, Hochberg, BH, BY or no correction.

    Full function reference: <https://github.com/KoliStat/the-stats-duck>.
