    DuckIR is a DuckDB extension for Information Retrieval. Vector Search is ready, and Full Text Search and Hybrid Search are on their way.

    Why DuckIR:

    - **Vector search fully integrated with DuckDB**, from query execution and predicate pushdown to transactional correctness, checkpoints, and crash recovery.
    - **Fast vector indexing and search**. Index millions of vectors in seconds and search them in milliseconds.
    - **Out-of-core:** Create and search indexes larger than memory.
    - **Filtered search** on any predicate DuckDB can evaluate.
    - **Lightweight maintenance** of indexes alongside your table, with no full rebuilds.
    - **Morsel-driven parallelism**, one row group at a time, as DuckDB does.

    > **Status: Alpha. Indexes might need to be recreated after an extension update.**

    See our [documentation](https://github.com/cwida/DuckIR) for maintenance, persistence, out-of-core search, and the known limitations.
