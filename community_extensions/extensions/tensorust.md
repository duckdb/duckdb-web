  extended_description: |
    Reads the [Tensorust Data API](https://shop.tensorust.com), which is built from public
    filings: SEC Business Development Company XBRL schedules of investments for private
    credit, and federal award records matched to 2025-26 papers for vendor exposure.

    | Function | One row per |
    | --- | --- |
    | `tensorust_lender_marks(company)` | BDC fund holding the company's first-lien loan, with its mark as a share of par |
    | `tensorust_credit_disagreements()` | loan that two or more BDC managers mark 10+ points apart |
    | `tensorust_q3_cuts()` | fund that cut a loan's mark by 5+ points from Jun 30 to Sep 30 2026 |
    | `tensorust_exposure(institution)` | federal award whose papers name a vendor on the DoD 1260H, Section 889, FCC Covered, UFLPA or BIOSECURE lists |
    | `tensorust_datasets(search := '', category := '')` | dataset in the catalog, with price tiers and sample link |
    | `tensorust_sample(slug)` | row of a dataset's free 25-row sample, with inferred column types |

    Columns are typed: marks and dollars are `DOUBLE`, counts `BIGINT`, dates `DATE`, and a
    figure the API does not publish stays `NULL`. Because the results are ordinary tables,
    a valuation team can join them to its own book in one query:

    ```sql
    SELECT b.borrower, b.my_mark, d.lowest_mark, d.highest_mark, d.gap_points
    FROM my_book b
    JOIN tensorust_credit_disagreements() d ON lower(d.company) = lower(b.borrower);
    ```

    Every function works without a key and returns the free preview (for example the top
    10 disagreements, or three funds per borrower). With an enterprise key the credit and
    exposure functions return full rows: every fund with par, prior-quarter mark, Sep 30
    mark and filing link, and every award with its evidence. Set it with
    `SET tensorust_api_key = 'tdk_...'` or the `TENSORUST_API_KEY` environment variable;
    it is sent as an `Authorization: Bearer` header, never in a URL. Each credit and
    exposure row has an `access` column (`preview` or `full`).
