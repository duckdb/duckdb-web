  extended_description: |
    `drain3` runs the Drain log parsing algorithm inside DuckDB. It groups log lines into templates
    such as `user <*> logged in from <*>` and extracts the variable parts. It is built on the
    [drain3](https://crates.io/crates/drain3) Rust crate, whose clusters and templates are identical
    to [logpai/Drain3](https://github.com/logpai/Drain3) on all 16 Loghub-2k datasets.

    - `drain_parse(table, message_column, order_by := ...)`: every row plus `cluster_id`,
      `template` and `params`.
    - `drain_train(message ORDER BY ts)`: an aggregate that returns the trained model as a BLOB.
    - `drain_match(model, message)`: matches a message without training. It runs in parallel.
    - `drain_clusters(model)`, `drain_params(template, message)`, `drain_render(template, params)`.

    Training is order-dependent. Use `ORDER BY` for results identical to logpai/Drain3. Defaults
    (`sim_threshold`, `depth`, `max_children`, `masks`, ...) match logpai/Drain3.
    See the [README](https://github.com/akshatagarwl/duckdb-drain3#readme) for examples, limits and
    benchmarks.
