    **healthkit_export** is a DuckDB scanner for exports produced by the

    **Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc.** Apple, Apple Health,
    and HealthKit are trademarks of Apple Inc.

    ### Table functions
    - `read_healthkit_export(path)` — top-level Health records
    - `healthkit_workouts(path)` — workouts
    - `healthkit_activity_summaries(path)` — daily activity rings
    - `healthkit_workout_routes(path)` — route metadata + GPX path
    - `healthkit_workout_route_points(path)` — GPX track points

    `path` may be `export.zip`, a directory containing `export.xml`, or `export.xml` itself.

    ### Design notes
    Large exports are a full **scan** (first pass can be memory-heavy). Prefer materialising
    slices with `COPY … TO '….parquet'` for day-to-day analytics.

    Top-level `<Record>` only — nested Correlation children are skipped (same samples usually
    also appear top-level).

    ### Platform
    Community builds target **DuckDB 2.0.0** on **macOS arm64**, **Linux**, and **Windows**.
    **Wasm** and **osx_amd64** are excluded. Prior CDN artifacts for DuckDB 1.5.x remain
    available for older clients until EOL.

    ### Rename note
    This listing **replaces** the short-lived `apple_health` community id (unintended branding).
    Use `INSTALL healthkit_export FROM community` going forward.

    https://github.com/Mjboothaus/duckdb-healthkit-export — optional Python helpers in-repo are
    **not** part of this extension binary.
