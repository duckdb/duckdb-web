  extended_description: |
    The usd extension reads Universal Scene Description (USD) stages - the scene format created by Pixar and used
    across 3D content, simulation and digital twin pipelines - and exposes their contents as tables:

    - `usd_prims`: the prims of a stage, with type, kind, activation, instancing, payloads, applied schemas,
      custom data and asset info
    - `usd_properties`: attributes and relationships, with typed values (VARIANT)
    - `usd_relationships`: relationship targets, for graph analysis
    - `usd_xforms`: world-space positions, rotations, scales and matrices
    - `usd_bounds`: world-space bounding boxes
    - `usd_variants`, `usd_layers`, `usd_stage_metadata` and `usd_composition_errors`: variant sets, the layers a
      stage uses, stage metadata such as units and up axis, and broken references
    - `usd_composition_arcs`: the references, payloads, inherits and variant selections of each prim, i.e. which
      assets a scene uses, where and how often
    - `usd_material_bindings`: the material each prim is bound to, and the binding that decides it
    - `usd_time_samples`: the values recorded over time - animation, sensor readings, simulation results

    Every function accepts a path, a glob or a list of paths, and options to restrict the scan to a subtree,
    descend into instances or skip payloads; values, transforms and bounds can be read at one or several time
    codes. Scans are parallel and only compute the columns a query uses, so scenes with millions of prims spread
    over hundreds of layers can be queried directly.

    OpenUSD 26.05 is linked into the extension, with its schemas for geometry, shading, lights, skeletons, physics,
    semantic labels, volumes, audio, render settings, procedurals and UI. See the
    [function reference](https://github.com/brannn/duckdb-usd/blob/main/docs/functions.md) and
    [cookbook](https://github.com/brannn/duckdb-usd/blob/main/docs/cookbook.md).
