PyData Amsterdam is a community conference organized by [NumFOCUS](https://numfocus.org/), bringing together users and developers of data science tools in the Python ecosystem.
The program consists of talks, tutorials and workshops on data analysis, machine learning and scientific computing.
DuckDB is a sponsor of the event.

## Venue

NDSM Loods, [Tt. Neveritaweg 15, 1033 WB Amsterdam](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6jV5LxYRKMKEgBhA9).

## Attendance

For the program, tickets and further details, visit the [PyData Amsterdam website](https://amsterdam.pydata.org/).
