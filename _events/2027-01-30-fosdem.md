> DuckLabs is [sponsoring this event](https://fosdem.org/2027/support/sponsors/).

[FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/2027/) is a free event for software developers to meet, share ideas, and collaborate. Every year, thousands of developers of free and open-source software from all over the world gather at the event in Brussels.

## Venue

The conference is held at the _ULB Solbosch Campus_, [Avenue Franklin Roosevelt 50, 1050 Brussels, Belgium (Google Maps)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ULB%20Solbosch%20Campus%20Brussels).

## Attendance

Attendance is free and no registration is required. For the schedule and further details, visit the [FOSDEM 2027 website](https://fosdem.org/2027/).
