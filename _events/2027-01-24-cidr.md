> DuckLabs is sponsoring this event.

The [Conference on Innovative Data Systems Research (CIDR)](https://www.cidrdb.org/cidr2027/) is a systems-oriented database conference, complementary to SIGMOD and VLDB, emphasizing the systems architecture perspective. The program consists of single-track keynote, paper and demo presentations, along with evening sessions including the famous gong show.

CIDR has a special place in DuckDB's history: following the SIGMOD 2019 demo paper [“DuckDB: An Embeddable Analytical Database”]({% link _library/2019-06-30-duckdb-sigmod-demo.md %}), which first introduced DuckDB publicly, the paper capturing DuckDB's vision, [“Data Management for Data Science Towards Embedded Analytics”]({% link _library/2020-01-12-embedded-analytics.md %}), was presented at CIDR 2020 in Amsterdam.

## Venue

The conference is held at the _Mövenpick Hotel Amsterdam City Centre_, [Piet Heinkade 11, 1019 BR Amsterdam, The Netherlands (Google Maps)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=M%C3%B6venpick%20Hotel%20Amsterdam%20City%20Centre).
