## Abstract

DuckDB is an open source analytical database system with a vibrant community of contributors and over a million downloads per day. The project started by Hannes Mühleisen and Mark Raasveldt at CWI, the Dutch national research institute for mathematics and computer science in Amsterdam. It then continued its course through DuckLabs, a spin-off company founded by the two creators, which was recently acquired by AWS.

In this talk, we’ll walk through DuckDB’s journey from an academic research project to a beloved tool that attracted the interest of major players in the area of data management. We’ll share lessons learned, anecdotes, as well as personal perspectives on why it’s a great example of research built around real user needs.
