Alex Kasko from DuckLabs will give a lightning talk at the [London PostgreSQL Meetup Group's “New Postgres Tools for Analytics” event](https://www.meetup.com/london-postgresql-meetup-group/events/316581290/). The talk is scheduled for 8:00 PM BST on October 7, 2026.

## Attendance

The organizers ask attendees to register on the [Meetup page](https://www.meetup.com/london-postgresql-meetup-group/events/316581290/).

## Abstract

DuckDB is an in-process analytical database, often described as “SQLite for analytics.” In this talk, Alex explains how DuckDB's `postgres` extension is built and how it runs parallel analytical queries against PostgreSQL data.
