The DuckDB Paris meetup is hosted by DuckDB and Altertable at Algolia’s Paris office, and features talks, demos, food and drinks.

## Venue

Algolia, 55 Rue d’Amsterdam, 75008 Paris.

## Program

The meetup runs from 18:30 to 22:30.

| Presenter          | Affiliation | Title                                                 |
| :----------------- | :---------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| Tom Larkworthy     | Taktile     | DuckDB-Iceberg at Taktile                             |
| Sylvain Utard      | Altertable  | DuckDB, dbt, Death of Pipeline                        |
| Christophe Blefari | Nao Labs    | Why DuckDB Is the Missing Piece of Your Agentic Setup |
