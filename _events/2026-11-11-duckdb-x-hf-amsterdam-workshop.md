## AI-Powered Review Analysis with DuckDB and Hugging Face: Build an Automated Customer Feedback Pipeline

> **HANDS-ON WORKSHOP — BRING YOUR LAPTOP!**

Ratings are slipping, thousands of reviews are piling up, and nobody knows why. In this hands-on workshop, you'll build a pipeline that finds out automatically.

Working with **2.1 million real Amazon reviews** from the Hugging Face Hub, you'll use **DuckDB** to pinpoint which products are declining. Then you'll connect that SQL to an LLM with Gradio's `gr.Workflow`, so the model reads only the complaints that matter and reports the themes behind them. Finally, you'll deploy it as a live **Hugging Face Space**, backed by Storage Buckets and callable as an API.

You'll leave with a working app and a pattern you can apply to your own customer data!
