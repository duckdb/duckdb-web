> DuckLabs is sponsoring this event.

[PyCon NL](https://www.pycon-nl.org/) is the Dutch Python community conference, bringing together Python developers, data scientists, and enthusiasts for talks, tutorials, and networking.

## Venue

The conference is held at _Jaarbeurs Utrecht_, [Jaarbeursplein 6, 3521 AL Utrecht, The Netherlands (Google Maps)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DNnoM67prnRh3tPP9).

## Attendance

For the program, tickets and further details, visit the [PyCon NL website](https://www.pycon-nl.org/).
