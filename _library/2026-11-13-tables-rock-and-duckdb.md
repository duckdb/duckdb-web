## Description

Hannes Mühleisen joins Juan Sequeda and Tim Gasper to discuss why tables keep coming back and how the pendulum always swings. They cover the background of DuckDB, how it started, its internals, and when to use it and when not to use it. Further topics include scale-up versus scale-out, the governance of local compute, AI agents, building a database company, and whether the world needs more database systems.
