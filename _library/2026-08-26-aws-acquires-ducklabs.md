## Description

This is a breaking news episode of *It's About Data*, responding to the announcement that Amazon has signed an agreement to acquire DuckLabs, the developer of the DuckDB analytics database.

Hosts Matt Housley and Tony Baer react to the news and discuss what it could mean for DuckDB's roadmap and open-source governance, drawing parallels to earlier moments in the ecosystem, including the founding of MotherDuck and Databricks' acquisition of the Neon/Lakebase team.

*It's About Data* is a weekly podcast from dbInsight and Halfpipe Systems offering straight-talking commentary on data, AI, and analytics technology, with special episodes released when major industry news breaks.
