## Abstract

Jake Thomas shares insights from Okta's embedded OLAP journey, exploring how the company shifted from traditional data processing systems to embedded database systems across multiple workloads. Whether you're considering embedded analytics or rethinking your data architecture, this talk offers valuable perspectives on the foundational shift to mini serverless databases and the re-entry of databases into application engineering domains.
