## Abstract

For the past 20 years, the data community has been building analyses to help people everywhere. As AI transforms how people work, it’s time to ask: what do we want the future of analytics to be? In this keynote, Christophe Blefari shares ideas and experiments on where the ecosystem should go. He starts by looking back at the roots of analytics and why it exists in the first place, then showcases a series of experiments that apply agentic (and non-agentic) approaches to analytics, illustrating how these emerging patterns could redefine the way we interact with data.
