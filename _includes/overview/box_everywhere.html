Runs everywhere

DuckDB has no external dependencies and compiles to WebAssembly, so it also runs in the browser – try it at shell.duckdb.org.

DuckDB runs everywhere: on phones, on single-board computers such as the Raspberry Pi, and on laptops and servers with Linux, macOS, and Windows.
